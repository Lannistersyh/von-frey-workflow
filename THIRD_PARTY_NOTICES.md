# Sources and third-party material

The public entry panel and legacy preview module are curated copies of the local project's software. Research records and archived reference material are retained in the encrypted original-project snapshot.

Method citations:

- Chaplan SR, Bach FW, Pogrel JW, Chung JM, Yaksh TL. *Quantitative assessment of tactile allodynia in the rat paw.* Journal of Neuroscience Methods. 1994;53(1):55–63. https://doi.org/10.1016/0165-0270(94)90144-9
- Christensen SL, et al. *Von Frey testing revisited: Provision of an online algorithm for improved accuracy of 50% thresholds.* European Journal of Pain. 2020;24:783–790. https://doi.org/10.1002/ejp.1528

The external calculator is https://bioapps.shinyapps.io/von_frey_app/. Its upstream R source is https://github.com/MikkelAStorm/Up.Down.Method; the package DESCRIPTION declares the GNU General Public License. That R implementation, the local translation derived from it, and the archived paper PDF/text are not redistributed as plaintext in this showcase. Their original local copies are preserved within the encrypted private snapshot for the project owner's recovery.
